Method

The 4 filters we applied

Out of 60+ PCM career options in coaching brochures, only those passing all four filters made the cut.

1. Income floor
Entry > Rs 5 LPA OR clear path to Rs 15 LPA in 7 years. Below this, cannot insulate 4-member family from inflation in tier-1 city by 2035.
2. AI-resistance
Will it exist in recognisable form in 2035? Three sub-tests - physical/manual presence, legal accountability, high-stakes judgement under uncertainty.
3. Foreign portability
Can you earn USD/EUR/AUD/CAD/AED with clear visa path? Hedges INR depreciation (~50% loss vs USD over 15 yrs).
4. Family stability stack
Salary + medical insurance + pension/EPF + housing loan + child school admission + community respect. Salary alone is not stability.

Action roadmap

Year by year - Class 11 to first job

Lock the foreign-option exam early. Save 30%+ of first salary. Foreign move ideally before age 30 (visa age points).

Class 11 - now

  • Lock subjects - PCM + ideally B (PCMB) for medicine optionality
  • Start JEE Main + Advanced prep (Allen / Resonance / FIITJEE / online)
  • Take aptitude test - confirm fit before locking direction
  • Begin English certification path - spoken English + IELTS familiarity

Class 12

  • JEE Main (Jan + April), JEE Advanced (May), BITSAT, state CETs
  • If medicine - NEET in May
  • If Defence - NDA written (April + Sept) and SSB
  • If Aviation - DGCA Class 1 medical, start CPL admission process

College year 1-2

  • Build portfolio - GitHub for CS, projects + internships for engineering
  • Lock foreign-option exam early - GRE for USA, IELTS for Canada/Australia, German A1-B2
  • Decide GATE vs MBA vs job track by end of Year 2

College year 3-4

  • Internships in target sector - 2-3 substantial ones
  • If GATE/PSU route - prep starts Year 3
  • If higher studies abroad - applications Year 4 Sep-Dec
  • If campus placement - target tier-1 firms with AI/ML/cybersec specialisation

First 5 years of work

  • Save 30%+ of income, 6-month emergency fund, term insurance, parents' health insurance
  • Foreign move ideally before age 30
  • Domain depth > job-hopping. Become known in one sub-field
